What % of polygons fit inside a fixed tile size — and how wasteful is that tile?

Coverage (blue) is the % of the 253 polygons whose full bounding box fits inside a given fixed tile size. Background waste (orange) is the median share of that tile's pixels that are not the polygon — i.e. polygon area ÷ tile area, per polygon, median across all polygons that fit — the class-imbalance cost of using a tile bigger than a given polygon actually needs.
